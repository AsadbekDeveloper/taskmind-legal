Privacy policy

TaskMind · Last updated: 2026-11-15

Who we are

TaskMind is a task and project management app with AI assistance, published by Asadbek (“we”). Contact: {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, name and password. The password is handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see it.
  • Profile (optional): role, bio, skills, status, language and theme.
  • Your content: projects, columns, tasks, comments, time estimates and logged time.
  • AI requests: text you send to an AI feature (see below).
  • Reports: when you report content, the reported text, the reason and your user id.
  • Diagnostics: crash and error reports (stack traces, device model, Android version, app version) with an anonymous user id, collected by Firebase Crashlytics. They contain no email or name.
  • Device integrity: Firebase App Check uses Google Play Integrity to confirm the app is genuine. Google receives an attestation from your device for this.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers, and the app shows no ads.

How we use it

To run the app (sign-in, saving and syncing your projects), to provide AI features, to review reports of abusive content, to find and fix crashes, and to keep the service secure. We do not sell your data.

AI features

When you use Smart Fill, task breakdown, description generation, the status report or the AI chat, the text needed for that request is sent to Google Gemini through Firebase AI Logic. This can include task titles and descriptions, project and column names, member names and your chat messages. It is processed by Google under its Firebase and Gemini API terms. AI answers can be wrong or inappropriate: check them, and report them with the flag button. AI features run only after you accept the notice shown on first use.

Who can see your data

Members of a project you belong to can see that project’s content and your name, email and profile details. Other people can add you to a project only if they know your exact email address; the app has no public user directory.

Service providers

We use Google’s Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, App Check, Crashlytics, AI Logic) and Google Play. Your data is stored and processed on Google’s servers, which may be outside your country. We use no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete account) or on the account deletion page. Crash reports are kept by Firebase for a limited period. Reports you filed may be kept so that abuse can be reviewed.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and access to the database is limited by security rules to the people who belong to a project. No system is perfectly secure.

Children

TaskMind is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

You can see and edit your profile in the app and delete your account at any time. To ask about or correct other data, email us.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date above and, where required, tell you in the app.
