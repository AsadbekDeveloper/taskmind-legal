Delete your TaskMind account

TaskMind · Last updated: 2026-11-15

In the app

  1. Open Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.
  3. Enter your password and tap Delete my account.

What is deleted

  • Your account and profile.
  • The comments you wrote.
  • Every project you own, with its tasks, columns and comments, for all of its members.
  • Your membership in projects owned by others (you are removed from them).

What is kept

  • Tasks you created in projects owned by other people stay in those projects.
  • Reports you filed about content, with your user id, so abuse can be reviewed.
  • Crash reports and backups held by Google for a limited period.

Cannot use the app?

Email asadbekdeveloper@gmail.com from the address of your account with the subject “Delete my account”. We will delete it within 30 days.
